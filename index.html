Copywriter • Brand strategy • Consumer psychology

I study the category before I write the line.

I’m Samin Zaman. I like to understand the market, the customer, and the psychology behind the purchase before I write anything. I care about clear, persuasive copy that makes a product feel obvious, desirable, and worth paying for.

I work with brands that need sharper positioning, better language, and messaging that feels precise instead of inflated. This portfolio includes concept work across menswear and skincare to show how I turn category insight into conversion-focused copy.
